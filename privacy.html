Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026

Prime AI Consultants LLC ("Prime," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect through this website, how we use and disclose it, and the choices available to you. This Policy covers the public marketing website only. It does not describe data handling under separate written consulting agreements.

1. Information you provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through the contact form or by email, including:

  • Name
  • Work email address
  • Company
  • Role or job title
  • Primary system or technology environment
  • Desired project timing or timeline
  • Information included in an inquiry or other communication

As of the effective date, the public contact form does not request a phone number. If you choose to call or email us, we may retain the contact details you share.

Please do not submit passwords, production data, trade secrets, personal information relating to other people, regulated information, security-sensitive information, or other confidential material through the public contact form. We mean that.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use the website, our hosting and related providers may automatically process technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring page
  • Pages viewed
  • Date and time of access
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Website performance, security, and interaction data

As of the effective date, Prime does not use a separate analytics product (for example, Google Analytics) on this website. Hosting is provided by Vercel. Fonts and other static assets may be delivered through third-party content networks (currently Google Fonts), which may receive technical request data such as IP address when pages load.

Contact-form submissions are transmitted to Formspree (formspree.io), which processes the submission and delivers it to Prime (typically by email to our business inbox). Formspree may also process technical metadata associated with the submission, such as IP address, consistent with its own practices.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Evaluate and respond to inquiries
  • Communicate about potential or existing business relationships
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website
  • Maintain appropriate business and legal records
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect rights

4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to:

  • Service providers acting on our behalf, including Vercel (hosting), Formspree (contact-form processing), and email or communications providers used to receive and respond to inquiries
  • Other hosting, security, font/CDN, and professional-service providers needed to operate the website
  • Advisors, insurers, accountants, auditors, and legal counsel
  • Government authorities or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of our services
  • A successor or participant in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law

We do not sell contact-form information, and we do not disclose it for third-party direct marketing.

5. Sale and sharing

Prime does not sell personal information for money. The website does not currently use advertising, cross-context behavioral analytics, or retargeting pixels. If that changes, this Policy will be updated and any required choice mechanisms will be implemented.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not intentionally use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. Our providers may use strictly necessary technologies to operate, secure, and deliver the website. The site may use browser local storage for optional preference storage on your device (for example, dismissing a temporary announcement if one is shown).

7. Retention

We retain information as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including responding to inquiries, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and complying with legal obligations. Retention periods vary by the type of information, purpose, sensitivity, and applicable requirements.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission, storage, or security is completely secure. Transmission of form data to Formspree and delivery to our inbox involve third-party systems outside Prime's sole control.

9. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted, subject to applicable law and appropriate verification. You may also ask questions about our privacy practices.
