Effective date

July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prime AI Consultants LLC (“Prime,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the public website at primeaiconsultants.com (the “Site”).

1. Information you provide

We may collect information you choose to provide through an inquiry form, email, telephone call, or other communication, such as your name, work email, company, role, system environment, and the content of your message.

Do not submit confidential, proprietary, regulated, personal, export-controlled, or security-sensitive information through the public Site.

2. Information collected automatically

The Site and its service providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver, secure, and understand use of the Site, such as IP address, browser and device information, referring page, pages requested, timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs.

The exact information depends on the hosting, form, security, and analytics services enabled at the time of your visit.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • evaluate and respond to inquiries;
  • communicate about a potential or existing business relationship;
  • operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site;
  • prevent misuse, fraud, or security incidents;
  • maintain appropriate business and legal records;
  • comply with law and protect rights, safety, and property.

4. Service providers

We may use service providers for website hosting, form processing, communications, security, analytics, and related operations. These providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy practices.

The Site currently uses Vercel for hosting and may use Formspree for inquiry-form processing.

5. How information may be disclosed

We may disclose information:

  • to service providers supporting the purposes described above;
  • to professional advisers when reasonably necessary;
  • when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request;
  • to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of Prime or others;
  • in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal information for money. Counsel must confirm whether any enabled analytics or advertising practice constitutes “selling,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under applicable law.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including business, legal, security, and recordkeeping needs. Retention periods vary by the type of information and context.

Prime uses reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of transmission, processing, or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information. To ask a privacy question or submit a request, email hello@primeaiconsultants.com.

Prime may need to verify your identity and may retain information about the request as required or permitted by law.
