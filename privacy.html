Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Prime AI Consultants LLC ("Prime," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect through this website, how we use and disclose it, and the choices available to you. This Policy covers the public marketing website only. It does not describe data handling under separate written consulting agreements.

1. Information you provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through the contact form or by email, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Business name
  • Phone number
  • Industry
  • Information included in an inquiry or other communication

The public contact form requests your name and email address, and you may optionally add your business name, a phone number, your industry, and a message. If you choose to call or email us, we may retain the contact details you share.

Please do not submit passwords, production data, trade secrets, personal information relating to other people, regulated information, security-sensitive information, or other confidential material through the public contact form. We ask you to keep that material offline until an agreement is in place.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use the website, our hosting and related providers may automatically process technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring page
  • Pages viewed
  • Date and time of access
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Website performance, security, and interaction data

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand how the website is used and how quickly pages load. Both are provided by Vercel, our hosting provider, and are designed to work without cookies and without cross-site tracking. We use them to measure aggregate, non-identifying information such as page views, the pages visited, referring sources, approximate geographic region, device and browser categories, and page performance measurements such as load timing. This information is reported to us in aggregate. We do not use it to identify individual visitors, and we do not combine it with information you submit through the contact form.

Vercel processes this information on our behalf as our hosting provider. Vercel's own privacy documentation describes how it handles data for these services. We do not use Google Analytics or any comparable third-party advertising or behavioral analytics product on this website. Fonts and other static assets may be delivered through third-party content networks (currently Google Fonts), which may receive technical request data such as IP address when pages load.

The public contact form is processed by Formspree, a form service that transmits your submission to our business inbox. Formspree processes the information you enter for that purpose, subject to its own privacy practices. We receive your submission by email and handle it as described in this policy.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Evaluate and respond to inquiries
  • Communicate about potential or existing business relationships
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website
  • Maintain appropriate business and legal records
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect rights

4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to:

  • Service providers acting on our behalf, including Vercel (hosting) and email or communications providers used to receive and respond to inquiries
  • Other hosting, security, font/CDN, and professional-service providers needed to operate the website
  • Advisors, insurers, accountants, auditors, and legal counsel
  • Government authorities or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of our services
  • A successor or participant in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law

We do not sell contact-form information, and we do not disclose it for third-party direct marketing.

5. Sale and sharing

Prime does not sell personal information for money. The website does not use advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, or retargeting pixels. The analytics service described in Section 2 does not use cookies and does not track visitors across other websites. If our practices change, this Policy will be updated and any required choice mechanisms will be implemented.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. Our providers may use strictly necessary technologies to operate, secure, and deliver the website. The analytics service described in Section 2 is designed to operate without cookies and without persistent identifiers, and it reports aggregated measurements rather than individual visitor profiles. The site may use browser local storage for optional preference storage on your device (for example, dismissing a temporary announcement if one is shown).

We retain information as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including responding to inquiries, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and complying with legal obligations. Retention periods vary by the type of information, purpose, sensitivity, and applicable requirements.
