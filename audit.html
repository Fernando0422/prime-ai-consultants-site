Find out what AI says about your business.

The problem

Your next customer might ask an AI before they ever see your website.

People already ask AI tools for recommendations the way they used to ask Google. When someone types "best plumber in Santa Clarita" into ChatGPT, the answer comes from what the AI knows about local businesses. If your phone number is wrong, your reviews are thin, or a same-named business in another state shows up first, you lose the job before you knew it existed.

Most local businesses have never checked. We have, and most score under 60 out of 100.

What the audit does

Nine real customer questions, three AI tools, one score.

We run your business through the same questions your customers ask, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google, and score what comes back across five dimensions:

  • Presence. Does your business show up at all?
  • Accuracy. Are the phone number, hours, and details right?
  • Sentiment. What tone does the AI use about you?
  • Citation quality. Does it point to your site or someone else's?
  • Competitive share. Do competitors get recommended instead of you?
What you get

A plain-English report, not a dashboard.

Your score out of 100, the exact quotes from each AI tool, and the fixes ranked by impact. No jargon, no 40-page PDF. You read it in ten minutes and know what to fix first. The fixes are usually straightforward: one correct phone number everywhere, review volume, clear local naming.

How it works

Three steps, report within a week.

  • 1. We run the queries. Nine buying-intent queries about your business across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google.
  • 2. We score the answers. Five dimensions, 0 to 100, recorded exactly as the tools answered.
  • 3. You get the report. Score, quotes, and fixes ranked by impact, with a walkthrough of what to fix first.
