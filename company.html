Why we exist

Manufacturing AI fails when it's sold by people who've never been inside the operation.

Money goes into models, platforms, and interfaces. The context underneath stays fragmented. Schemas without meaning. Reports that don't match. Knowledge stuck in people's heads.

That's why we started this firm. Antonio spent nearly two decades inside semiconductor manufacturing applications. So we approach MES, ERP, and adjacent systems the same way he learned to: recover meaning first. Talk about AI after.

  • Context first Systems, definitions, and rules before anyone builds
  • Founder-led Floor experience paired with how the work feels to clients
  • Careful claims We won't stretch the marketing ahead of the evidence

What we stand on

A few things we won't bend on.

Built inside operations, not from a whitepaper

The methods come from long experience inside manufacturing and enterprise applications. Real constraints. Real pressure to get definitions right when two reports disagree.

Governed access or no access

We won't recommend connecting AI to unrestricted raw operational tables. Proposed paths run through approved views, client review, and traceable access.

You own what we build for you

Client-specific dictionaries, documentation, views, and related materials are delivered under the ownership and licensing terms in the written agreement. You shouldn't need a platform lock-in to keep using them.

We report what we find

If discovery shows the blocker is process, ownership, or data quality rather than model selection, we say so. Confirmed facts, inferences, and unresolved questions stay labeled separately. This is the boring part on purpose.

A useful conversation can start before a project does.

Tell us which environment you're in and which decision needs clarity. Often one short conversation is enough to see whether a focused engagement makes sense.
