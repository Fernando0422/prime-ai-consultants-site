Why we exist

Manufacturing AI projects struggle when they're sold by people who've never been inside the operation.

Money goes into models, platforms, and interfaces while the context underneath stays fragmented: schemas without meaning, reports that don't match, and knowledge stuck in people's heads.

That's why we started this firm. Antonio has spent more than three decades bridging enterprise technology and operations, including ERP, CRM, and Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) across the United States and Mexico. So we approach those environments the same way he learned to: recover meaning first, then talk about AI.

  • Context first Systems, definitions, and rules before anyone builds
  • Founder-led Floor experience paired with how the work feels to clients
  • Careful claims We won't stretch the marketing ahead of the evidence

  • Service and experience Fernando structures the service, the research, the communication, and how it feels to work with us.
  • Documented limits Assumptions, unresolved questions, ownership, and limitations go in writing. If they aren't written, we stop.

What we stand on

A few things we won't bend on.

Built inside operations, not from a whitepaper

The methods come from long experience inside manufacturing and enterprise applications, under real constraints and real pressure to get definitions right when two reports disagree.

Governed access or no access

We won't recommend connecting AI to unrestricted raw operational tables. Proposed paths run through approved views, client review, and traceable access.

You own what we build for you

Client-specific dictionaries, documentation, views, and related materials are delivered under the ownership and licensing terms in the written agreement. You shouldn't need a platform lock-in to keep using them.

We report what we find

If discovery shows the blocker is process, ownership, or data quality rather than model selection, we say so. Confirmed facts, inferences, and unresolved questions stay labeled separately. That careful labeling is intentional.
