Accessibility

Prime AI Consultants wants its public website to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

Our approach

We aim to support:

  • keyboard navigation;
  • clear focus indicators;
  • logical headings and landmarks;
  • readable contrast and text sizing;
  • meaningful link and button labels;
  • form labels, instructions, and error messages;
  • alternative text for informative images;
  • reduced-motion preferences where motion is used;
  • responsive layouts across common screen sizes.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing practice. Content, browsers, devices, assistive technologies, and site features change over time. We review the site and work to address barriers we identify.

This statement does not claim certification or complete conformance with a particular standard unless expressly stated following a qualified review.
