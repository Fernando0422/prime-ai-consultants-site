AI for enterprise resource planning systems

An ERP transaction is not the same as an operational explanation.

ERP systems record orders, inventory, costs, receipts, shipments, customers, vendors, and financial events. Years of configuration, custom fields, integrations, reports, and reconciliation practices determine what those records mean in your organization.

Prime documents that context before AI is asked to explain or act on it.

The calculation often extends beyond the schema.

A finance or operational answer may depend on:

  • master-data conventions and historical changes;
  • posting, allocation, and reconciliation rules;
  • custom fields, extensions, and interfaces;
  • scheduled jobs and extracts;
  • spreadsheet adjustments and report logic;
  • timing, close, approval, and exception practices;
  • ownership split across finance, operations, IT, and external partners.

A plausible answer is not enough. Stakeholders need to understand the source, logic, exceptions, and validation path.

Start with one ERP-centered decision.

  • Which modules, entities, reports, interfaces, and periods shape the answer?
  • Which calculation or reconciliation rules live outside standard configuration?
  • Where do custom fields, extracts, mappings, or manual adjustments change meaning?
  • Which source is authoritative for the selected decision?
  • Who owns the definition, the source, and the validation?
  • What access is appropriate for evaluation?
  • What should remain restricted?
  • What evidence would a future AI-assisted answer need to show?
  • system, module, report, and stakeholder inventory;
  • priority entity and transaction-flow map;
  • business definition and calculation register;
  • reconciliation, exception, and adjustment log;
  • source-of-record and ownership matrix;
  • access and restriction findings;
  • proposed validation path;
  • recommended next-step scope.

Questions worth documenting before automation.

  • Why do two inventory reports disagree?
  • Which rules determine available-to-promise for this operation?
  • How is a margin, backlog, or fulfillment measure actually calculated?
  • Which adjustments explain a reconciliation difference?
  • What evidence should accompany an AI-generated variance explanation?

These examples are prompts for discovery, not claims of completed Prime engagements or promised outcomes.

Start with the transaction flow and the decision.
