Where audits start

Patterns that keep coming up

The same few patterns show up for a lot of local businesses. None of them mean the work is not good. They mean customers cannot find, check, or reach the business as easily as they should.

Pattern 01

Details that do not line up

Basic information can drift out of sync across the places a business appears online. When what a customer finds does not match, they hesitate, or they move on to the next option.

Pattern 02

Good work that is hard to see

A business can have a strong local reputation and still be hard for search and AI tools to understand and recommend. If the record is not clear online, it does not get the credit it has earned.

Pattern 03

Proof that is scattered

Happy customers often leave feedback in more than one place, so a new buyer rarely sees the full picture in one view. Scattered proof is easy to miss, even when there is plenty of it.

Pattern 04

Inquiries that go quiet

Interest cools fast when a reply takes a while or the next step is not clear. Many businesses lose work here, not on quality, but in the gap between someone reaching out and someone getting back to them.

Questions

Questions owners ask us

What does the audit cost?

Nothing. The free audit is available to local businesses in the Santa Clarita Valley, the San Fernando Valley, and Los Angeles, and there is no charge to run it, review it, or keep the findings.

We check everywhere customers find you: in search, on your website, and in AI answers. You can take the findings and act on them yourself, hand them to someone else, or hire us to fix them. The audit does not commit you to a website, a package, or any paid work after the call.

What do I get from the audit call?

A clear picture of where your business is losing calls and what to fix first. On the call we walk you through your score, the specific findings behind that score, and the order in which those findings matter most for new jobs.

The audit looks at who Google and AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, etc) recommend instead of you, what customers see first on your Google profile, whether your phone number is the same everywhere, how your website works on a phone, whether buyers can see your best reviews in one place, and what happens after someone tries to reach you. The call runs about 15 minutes and stays in plain language, with no technical talk you have to translate later. If nothing is worth fixing, we will tell you that instead of selling you work you do not need.

I already have a website. Is this still for me?

Usually yes, and this is one of the most common situations we see in Santa Clarita, Valencia, Castaic, Canyon Country, Newhall, and Saugus. Having a website is not the same as having a website that brings in calls.

Many sites look fine on a desktop and still lose jobs because the phone number is wrong in one place, the hours are missing or out of date, the services you want to sell have no page of their own, or the neighborhoods you serve are never named on the site at all. Other sites load slowly on a phone, bury the call button, or give Google and AI tools too little clear information to recommend the business with confidence. The free audit shows which of those problems your site has, which ones are costing you calls right now, and whether a small fix is enough or a rebuild would serve you better.

Why is my business not showing up on Google or in ChatGPT answers?

Most often it is not because your work is weak. It is because the information search tools can find about your business is incomplete, unclear, or not consistent from place to place.

When someone nearby searches Google or asks ChatGPT who to call for a plumber, a roofer, a restaurant, or a realtor, those tools recommend businesses they can understand quickly and trust. That trust comes from a complete Google profile with correct hours, categories, services, and photos, a website that clearly names your services and the areas you serve, one phone number that matches everywhere, and reviews that are easy to find in one view. If any of those pieces conflict or go missing, Google and AI answers will often name a competitor whose information is clearer, even when your reviews and your work are stronger. The audit checks each of those pieces in the order a customer meets them.

Will you use AI in my business?

Where it makes sense for your business, yes. We provide AI solutions built around the specific problems your business has, not a standard package that gets dropped into every shop, office, or trade business. That is exactly where the consulting work comes in.

During the audit and the consult, we find where you are losing time, losing calls, or losing follow up, and then decides with you whether AI is the right tool for that problem at all. For one business that might mean help with missed calls after hours. For another it might mean a faster first reply to new inquiries, a better way to answer repeat questions, or a small tool that sorts new jobs before they reach you.

Our ai work is additive and never replaces the systems you already trust. Your current setup stays the source of truth, new tools connect to it and log back into it, and call help starts after hours or in shadow mode with a clear way to switch it off. If a simpler fix will solve the problem for less, we will recommend that simpler fix instead.

I am a realtor. My broker controls my listings.

That is normal for realtors in Castaic, Valencia, and across the Santa Clarita Valley, and it shapes what we can and cannot build for you. Your broker controls your listings and the official listing pages, and each brokerage sets its own rules for personal marketing, websites, and approval.

We do not know if your brokerage allows a personal page under the brokerage name until those rules are checked, so we check first and never promises that page in advance. In most cases your own website is the better option anyway. You own it and control it, it can focus on your name, your sales record, and the neighborhoods you know best, and it stays with you if you change brokerages.

Any site we build for a realtor carries your name, your license number, and your broker, can show a few featured listings with links out to the official broker listings, and is updated manually rather than presented as a live listing feed. Nothing goes live without broker approval.
