About Prime

A small firm with a documented first step.

Prime AI Consultants is a founder-led, father-and-son firm. We help operational teams document the systems, definitions, business rules, ownership, and controls behind important decisions before anyone commits to a larger AI build.

Meet the founders

Operational AI needs people who understand the operation.

Organizations invest in models, platforms, and interfaces while the context underneath remains fragmented: schemas without shared meaning, reports that disagree, custom logic with no clear owner, and knowledge that never made it into documentation.

Prime was created to begin earlier. Recover the context. Label what is known and unknown. Give stakeholders something concrete to review. Talk about implementation only when the evidence supports it.

Father and son. Different responsibilities. Shared accountability.

Antonio Rojas

Co-Founder, Enterprise Applications and Operational Systems

Antonio brings more than three decades of experience in enterprise applications and IT, including applications leadership in semiconductor manufacturing since 2007. His background includes work with ERP, manufacturing applications, integrations, databases, reports, and the operational logic that accumulates around long-running systems.

At Prime, Antonio leads operational discovery, business-logic reconstruction, technical review, and delivery standards. He works across the boundary between system owners, database teams, engineers, operators, and business stakeholders.

Antonio’s employer experience is presented solely as professional background. Prime does not claim endorsement by his current or former employers and does not use confidential employer information, data, systems, or outcomes in its marketing.

Service and communication

Documented limits

Assumptions, disagreements, unresolved questions, ownership, dependencies, and restrictions go in writing.

Client authority

The client’s legal, privacy, security, compliance, data-owner, system-owner, and executive functions retain final approval.

A few things we will not bend on.

Context before implementation

Systems, definitions, rules, exceptions, owners, and validation come before a build.

Evidence before marketing

Prime will not invent case studies, results, partnerships, or credentials to appear further along.

Approved access or no access

Prime does not recommend unrestricted AI access to raw operational tables as a default.

Useful work should be portable

Client-specific materials are delivered under the ownership and licensing terms in the written agreement. Prime should not manufacture lock-in.

The recommendation may be to wait

If discovery shows the blocker is ownership, process, data quality, access, or an unresolved definition, Prime says so.

We would rather be precise than look larger.

Prime is early as a firm. Our public claims rest on the founders’ accurately described experience, the services we can deliver today, and the commitments we put in writing.
