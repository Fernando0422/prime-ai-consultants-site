Diagnostics

Two first steps, one clear picture

Every engagement starts by looking before anything gets built.

The first step takes two forms, and the right one depends on how your business runs.

The diagnostic

For manufacturers and for law and professional firms

We go in and meet the people who run the work. In a plant, that means the engineers, directors, and managers. In a firm, it means the partners and the team who handle intake and case work day to day.

We map how the work actually flows, where it slows down, where it gets handed off, and where money is lost along the way. Nothing is proposed until that picture is written down and agreed.

For law and professional firms, the diagnostic is free and takes 30 minutes. For manufacturers, it starts with a conversation about your environment.

Start with a diagnostic

The audit

For local businesses

We review your Google reviews and what they tell a customer who is deciding whether to call. We check how your business ranks when customers search for your kind of service.

We also ask AI who it recommends when a customer asks for that service, and whether your business is the name it gives. You see where you stand today, and what is costing you calls.

You get the same attention as any other business we work with. We will meet with you in person at your business to go through the findings together and talk through what to fix first.

The audit is free, and the findings are yours to keep either way.

Methodology

Write it down first, then build

The method is the same for any business, whether the work sits in a plant, a firm, or a shop. Four steps, in order, and each step earns the next one.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We write down how your business actually works. The systems, the handoffs, and the way a normal day really runs, in plain language, before anyone proposes a tool.

  2. 02

    Define

    We agree on the exact problem worth fixing and the order to fix it in. One system and one decision at a time, so the work stays clear and the scope stays honest.

  3. 03

    Govern

    We set the rules before anything is built. Which system stays the source of truth, what a new tool is allowed to touch, and what stays in human hands.

  4. 04

    Activate

    We design the architecture first, then carry out the implementation in the agreed order. You approve the scope and a written quote before work begins.

Add to what works

What already works stays in place. New work connects to your current systems and logs back into them, so nothing your business depends on gets pulled out from under it.

One system, one decision at a time

We do not rebuild everything at once. Each change is small enough to check, and each decision is written down before the next one is made.

You keep everything we build

The site, the content, the profiles, and the systems we build are yours to keep. If we stopped working together, it all stays with your business.

Questions

Questions owners ask us

What does the first step cost?

Nothing. The first audit or diagnostic is free, and it comes with a free 30-minute conversation. We go through what we found together, and more importantly, we find out where the exact problems are for your business. Your problem may not be in the audit at all. It may be something you are dealing with right now, and the audit is there to support that conversation, not replace it. You keep the findings either way, and nothing commits you to paid work. For a manufacturer, that first step is Prime Diagnostics, and it starts with a conversation about your environment.

Will you replace our current systems?

No. Our work is additive, never a replacement. Your current system stays the source of truth, and anything new connects to it and logs back into it.

How much does the work cost?

Pricing happens on the call, because the right scope depends on what your business actually needs. You get a written quote before any paid work starts, and you can say no to that quote and keep the findings either way.

Which businesses do you work with?

Any business. The industries we're focused on today are manufacturers, law and professional firms, and local businesses. Pick the industry above that fits, and that page shows the work in detail. If none of them fits, call and tell us about your business.

Do you only work in Santa Clarita?

No. The Santa Clarita Valley is home. Manufacturing and professional work reaches more broadly, and local business work is in person across the Santa Clarita Valley, the San Fernando Valley, and Los Angeles.
